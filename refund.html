Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

At RHEMA WAVE TECHNOLOGIES (BG Enterprises, LLC), we are committed to ensuring customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines our procedures for cancellations, returns, and refunds. Please note that all product purchases are governed by the specific terms of your individual Sales Agreement, which may contain additional or superseding provisions.

1. Sales Agreement Governs

All purchases of RHEMA WAVE TECHNOLOGIES systems are made pursuant to a formal Sales Agreement executed between the buyer and BG Enterprises, LLC. The refund, cancellation, and return terms specified in your Sales Agreement take precedence over this general policy. Please refer to your Sales Agreement for the specific terms applicable to your purchase.

2. Cancellation Policy

2.1 Prior to Production

If you wish to cancel your order before your system has entered production, please contact us immediately. Cancellations made before production begins may be eligible for a refund of your deposit, minus a restocking/administrative fee as specified in your Sales Agreement.

2.2 During Production

Once your system has entered production, cancellations may be subject to significant fees to cover materials, labor, and production costs already incurred. The specific terms will be outlined in your Sales Agreement.

2.3 After Delivery/Installation

Due to the custom nature and precision calibration of our systems, returns after delivery and/or installation are generally not accepted. Exceptions may be made at the sole discretion of the Company and will be subject to the terms of your Sales Agreement.

3. Warranty Claims

If your product has a manufacturing defect or malfunction covered under the warranty terms of your Sales Agreement, please contact our support team. Warranty claims are handled separately from the refund process and are subject to inspection and verification. See your Sales Agreement for full warranty terms and procedures.

4. Damaged or Defective Products

If your product arrives damaged or is found to be defective upon delivery:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs documenting the damage.
  • Do not attempt to install or use a visibly damaged system.
  • We will arrange for inspection, repair, or replacement at no additional cost for verified shipping damage or manufacturing defects.

5. Bio-Scalar Wearables & Accessories

Smaller products such as bio-scalar enhanced wearables and accessories may be returned within 30 days of purchase if they are unused, in original packaging, and in resalable condition. A restocking fee may apply. Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the buyer unless the product was defective or incorrectly shipped.

6. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund request or discuss cancellation:

  • Email: info@rhemawavetech.com
  • Include your name, order number or Sales Agreement reference, and reason for the request.
  • Our team will respond within 5 business days with next steps.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days of approval. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method unless otherwise agreed. Please allow additional time for your financial institution to process the refund.

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally non-refundable:

  • Installation and calibration service fees (once services have been rendered)
  • Licensing fees (once the license has been activated)
  • Custom configuration or build-out consultation fees
  • Shipping and handling costs (unless the return is due to our error)

9. Contact

For any questions about this Refund Policy, please contact us at info@rhemawavetech.com.
