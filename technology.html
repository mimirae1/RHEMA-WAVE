What Are Scalar Waves?

Scalar waves are a unique form of energy first theorized by James Clerk Maxwell in the 1860s and later demonstrated by Nikola Tesla. Unlike conventional electromagnetic waves that oscillate in a transverse pattern, scalar waves are longitudinal — they do not move in a linear direction and create a stable, standing energy field.

This field does not lose intensity over distance or time, making scalar energy fundamentally different from other forms of electromagnetic radiation. The result is a coherent, life-enhancing energy environment that can interact with biological systems at the most fundamental level.

Scalar fields are recognized in astrophysics, geology, and hydrodynamics. Our technology brings this natural phenomenon into a controlled, precision-engineered format for wellness applications.
